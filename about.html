The Story of the Bakery

North Star Bakery started out as a simple passion project in our family kitchen. Over time, we outgrew our home space and opened this small brick-and-mortar storefront to share our love for traditional baking with our neighbors.

Sourcing Philosophy

We care a lot about using great ingredients. We partner directly with regional grain mills to get organic flours, and we source our fruits and butter from nearby family farms. We never use artificial preservatives or fake flavorings.

Staff Highlights

Our small team is made up of passionate local bakers and friendly counter staff who love food. We are up at 3:00 AM every single day to ensure the shelves are completely packed with warm options right when our doors open at 6:00 AM.
